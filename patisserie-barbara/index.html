    Our story

    A small pâtisserie with a big hello

    On the Valkenburgerstraat, a short walk from Waterlooplein: peach walls, fresh flowers on the tables, a vitrine full of cakes. And Barbara herself behind the counter, saying hello and making your sandwich by hand.

    • Fresh from the ovenTime it right and your croissant comes straight out of the oven.
    • Lattes in colourPurple ube, green matcha, pink strawberry matcha. And a hot chocolate people write home about.
    • Sun on the terraceA few tables outside for a slow morning with coffee and a croissant.
