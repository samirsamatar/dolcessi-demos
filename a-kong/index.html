Sranang Snesie Koekroe

A time capsule on the Wagenaarstraat.

Eethuis A. Kong has been cooking Surinamese-Chinese food in the Dapperbuurt since 1997. It is a tiny place: a counter, a few tables, a window full of plants and a laminated menu that runs from 01 to 133.

Most people come for takeaway. The menu card is signed Leo & Ayun, and ends with a simple line:

“Hartelijk dank aan al onze klanten”Heartfelt thanks to all our customers
