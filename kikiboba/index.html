How we make it

From Batter to Wheel Cake

01麵Mix

A light batter, made fresh every day.

02烤Bake

Poured into the round cast-iron moulds until golden and crisp.

03餡Fill

Corn cheese, kimchi potato, ube or taro, sealed in the middle.

04吃Eat

Straight away, while it’s still warm. With a cup of tea on the side.

Our story

A Space for Ideas, Culture and Connection

01 · The space

Slow down, chill, share.

KIKIBOBA is a place to slow down and share moments together, with wheel cakes, tea, books and design under one roof.

02 · The name

Kiki is sharp, Bouba is soft.

Show people a spiky shape and a round one, and almost everyone calls the spiky one “kiki” and the round one “bouba”. We are a little of both.

✶kiki · sharp
●bouba · soft
03 · The window

Designed with friends.

Our window was designed by @chyi.tw, with type by @xleetype. Our first exhibition centres on Beryl Wu’s book Diary.
