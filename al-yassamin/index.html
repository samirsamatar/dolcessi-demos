Our story · ياسمين

Yassamin means jasmine

On the Eerste Oosterparkstraat, a long glass vitrine with gold trim runs through the shop. Behind it: trays of baklava and pistachio fingers, bird's nests, ma'amoul and barazek, laid out in neat rows on paper doilies, next to warm fatayer straight from the oven.

It's a patisserie and a bakery, and also a place to sit: breakfast, coffee, a glass of fresh mint tea or a juice, every day from eight in the morning.

Never had these sweets before? Just ask. As one guest put it: “a nice guy helped us choose.”
