ウキウキ(uki uki) · Japanese for happily excited

Named after that can't-wait feeling

Uki uki is the feeling on your way to dinner with friends. On the Binnen Bantammerstraat, a few steps from Nieuwmarkt and the Zeedijk, that feeling has a room of its own: copper lamps over a long wooden bar, cranes flying across the wall, and a kitchen cooking Korean and Japanese favourites.

Order on the tablet at your table and it arrives quickly: teppanyaki still sizzling, bubbling Korean stews, crispy fried chicken, ramen and hot pot to share. Save room for the matcha Basque cheesecake.
