Rozengracht 91 · Jordaan

A Greek coffee bar that opened with a rave.

Fanke threw open its doors on the Rozengracht with a Coffee Rave: 19 September 2026, ten till six, music by Mr. Tzi. The music stayed.

Inside: terracotta walls, a long wooden bar lined with chrome levers, and shelves of Greek wine, olive oil and deli finds at the back. The pies, bougatsa and pastries are made by hand.

ΦLook at the window: the F in Fanke is a Greek phi.
