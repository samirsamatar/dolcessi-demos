Bring the whole crew

Birthdays, bachelor parties and office nights. One room holds up to 25, all fourteen together hold 140, and the bar takes another 80. Tell us how many you are and see what fits.

How many of you?

Count everyone who's singing. We'll show the rooms that fit and what it costs.

Night

What are we celebrating?

Birthdays

Bring the cake. Mention it in the note field when you book, and bring your own plates and cutlery. Other food and drinks from outside aren't allowed.

Bachelor parties

Take one of the big rooms, Amsterdam for 12 to 15 or New York for 16 to 25, and share a 1.5-litre pitcher of Heineken for €21 while you work through the set list.

Office nights

Present on the 125-inch screen, then hand out the microphones. Book several rooms side by side, or the whole place: fourteen rooms and a bar for 80.
