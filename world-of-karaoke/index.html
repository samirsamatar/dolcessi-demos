How a night here works

  1. Pick your city and night

    Rooms for two up to twenty-five, €19 or €23 each for two hours. You book and pay on worldofkaraoke.nl and can move it once, up to 72 hours before.

  2. Queue your songs

    Search the catalogue on the touchscreen. Filter by decade or genre, and sing with guide vocals, without them, or over the original.

  3. Get a round in

    Scan the QR code in your room and a host brings cocktails, pitchers or soju to your door. The bar stays open until midnight, and until 02:00 on Friday and Saturday.

Good to know before you book

What does a room cost?

€19 per person on Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday. €23 per person on Friday and Saturday, for a two-hour session. You choose the number of people when you book and pay online in advance. The room comes with two wireless microphones and the full song catalogue; drinks are ordered separately.

How long is a session, and when do they start?

Every session is two hours. Each room has three sessions a night, starting between 16:30 and 21:45 depending on the room. On Friday and Saturday most rooms add a late session that runs past midnight.

How many people fit in a room?

Our smallest rooms, Paris and Istanbul, are for two or three. New York holds 16 to 25. Every room has a minimum and a maximum, so use the room finder above to see which ones fit your group.

Can we change or cancel a booking?

You can change a booking once, up to 72 hours before your session: the date, the session, the room or the number of people. Cancel at least 72 hours ahead and you get your money back minus a €5 administration fee. Discounted and tailor-made bookings can't be refunded.

How do drinks work, and is there food?

Every room has a QR code. Order on your phone and a karaoke host brings the drinks to your room. There is no food. The bar is open to everyone without a booking, and a table there can be requested by email.

We're with more than 25. Is that possible?

Yes. All fourteen rooms together hold up to 140 guests, and the bar area takes up to 80. For more than 25 people, a date outside opening hours or a custom request, email info@worldofkaraoke.nl.

What is the Chinese KTV system?

A separate karaoke system with a Chinese catalogue (中文 KTV 系统), for groups of four or more. Singapore, Hongkong and New York have it next to the regular system. Tokyo and Berlin run on it only, so choose Chinese KTV when you book those two.

Can we bring a birthday cake?

Yes. Mention it in the note field when you book, and bring your own plates and cutlery. Other food and drinks from outside aren't allowed.

Can we keep singing after two hours?

If nobody has booked your room for the next session, you can stay longer for an extra fee. If it is booked, the staff will look for another free room.

Can children come?

Yes. Guests under 16 need at least one parent or guardian (18+) with them. An early slot, such as a Saturday or Sunday afternoon, is the quietest.
