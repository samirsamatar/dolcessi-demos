Our story, in short

Pane e olio: bread and oil.

The simplest thing on an Italian table, and the name above a small door on the Oude Leliestraat, between the Singel and the Herengracht.

Inside it’s a Tuscan delicatessen, traiteur and takeaway in one. A glass case full of panini and focaccia, lasagna in the tray, cannoli and cornetti by the window, Italian espresso, and shelves of wine, olive oil and pasta.

There are a few tables if you’d like to stay. The owner loves a chat. And when the wisteria over the door is in bloom, the whole shopfront turns purple.
