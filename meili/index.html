Get to know Meili

From Chongqing ··· to the Haarlemmerdijk, Amsterdam

8,200kilometres from our hometown
274days from zero to opening day
1hand-lettered signboard

The journey from our hometown, Chongqing, to Amsterdam is around 8,200 kilometres. Starting from zero, it took us 274 days to turn our dream into a little Asian bakery on the Haarlemmerdijk.

Fun fact: our signboard is hand-lettered, just like our bread is hand-shaped. And if you’re craving something you saw somewhere on the internet, DM us. It might end up on the menu one day.
