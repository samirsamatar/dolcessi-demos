Buiksloterham · Amsterdam-Noord

Gentle by name,
gentle by nature.

Gentle Place Coffee is a specialty coffee bar on the Distelweg, in one of the newer corners of Amsterdam-Noord. Sage-green walls, a grey-brick bar, warm oak and big windows full of light and plants.

Come for just a drink, to get some work done or to catch up with someone. Order at the bar, find your spot and take your time.

  • Order at the bar
  • Wifi, no password
  • Oat milk & vegan banana bread
  • House-made tea blends
  • Tables outside on sunny days
