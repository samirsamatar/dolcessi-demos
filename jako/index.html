Our shop

A little pink shop on the Bos en Lommerweg.

Hot-pink walls, a ceiling full of balloons, a neon bubble-tea cup glowing on the wall and a rattan chair in the plant corner. Jako sits in Bos en Lommer, a little away from the centre, which is exactly why people call it a hidden gem.

The board holds 35 drinks, from brown sugar milk tea to mango cheese foam, plus snow ice and waffles. Reviewers mention the friendly people behind the counter, drinks ready in no time, and coming back almost every week.
