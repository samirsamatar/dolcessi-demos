    About Lapek

    Masakan khas Minang.

    Lapek cooks the food of the Minangkabau, from West Sumatra: the cuisine all of Indonesia simply calls ‘Padang’.

    Think slow-cooked beef rendang, coconut gulai, egg balado, cassava leaves and a green chilli sambal with a kick. Everything goes around a mound of white rice.

    The red roof on the window is a gonjong, the horned roof of a Minang rumah gadang. Inside: mustard-yellow walls, green booths, leafy wallpaper and round lamps.

    “…just like back home in Padang.”
