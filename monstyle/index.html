4ものがたり Story

A small shop
with a big window.

monstyle ams is a young, family-run coffee bar at Brouwersgracht 113, where the Jordaan meets the canal. Look for the round red sign with the white cup.

Inside: a terrazzo counter, chocolate-brown cups, pink tables and a window seat with one of the best views in town. Onigiri wait at the counter, the purin wobbles in its silver cup, and on the takeaway cups there is a little girl with a pearl earring.
