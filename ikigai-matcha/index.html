The story

A reason to get up on a Friday

Ikigai is a Japanese word for your reason for being: the thing that makes the day worth getting up for.

At IKIGAI Matcha on the Ferdinand Bolstraat, it comes in a tall cup. Kyushu matcha, homemade syrups and seasonal layers, made from scratch with real ingredients. Reviewers keep mentioning the same thing: a kind, passionate owner behind the bar.
