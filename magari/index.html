Our story

A Little Boutique on the Singel

01 · The place

Small, cosy, by the water.

Magari sits on one of the prettiest stretches of the Singel. A few tables inside, a terrace by the canal, and fresh flowers at the door.

02 · The name

마가리 · まがり · Magari

Our name is written in Korean, Japanese and Italian, just like the café brings a little of each to Amsterdam.

마가리Korean
まがりJapanese
03 · The details

Little things matter.

Antique cups, vintage interior pieces, a cat on the windowsill and a dog swinging over the counter. Everything here is chosen with care.
