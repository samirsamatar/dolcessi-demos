Good to know

How do I book a table?

Online through the booking page, by phone on +31 6 266 325 34 or by email at info@cafechacha.nl. The team confirms your booking.

Is the karaoke free?

Yes. Karaoke is free from Wednesday to Sunday from 21:00. To have the karaoke machine to yourselves, it is €20 per person for a group of at least 20, paid in advance. Call +31 6 266 325 34 for details.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes: the veggie burger, penne pesto, veggie sandwich, veggie salad, tomato soup, nachos, mini spring rolls and cheese sticks. They are marked with a leaf on the menu.

Can you host our group or event?

Yes. Lunch or dinner for up to 52 people downstairs, or 80 across both floors. Drinks and parties for up to 100. You can rent part of the café or all of it; ask for a proposal on the groups page.

Is there a terrace?

Yes, with heaters, so it is cosy on cooler days and evenings too.

Are there drinks without alcohol?

Yes: an alcohol-free Mojito or strawberry Mojito (€12), Pornstar Martini (€13) and Pink Gin & Tonic (€12), plus Heineken 0.0, non-alcoholic Radler, smoothies, fresh juice and coffee.

How late are you open?

Sunday to Wednesday from 11:00 to 22:00, Thursday to Saturday from 11:00 to 01:00.

How do I get there?

Metro 52 stops at Rokin station and trams 4 and 14 stop at Rokin. Dam Square is a few minutes on foot.
