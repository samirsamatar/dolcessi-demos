Good to know

Is the karaoke free?

Yes. From Wednesday to Sunday the karaoke starts at 21:00 and anyone in the café can sing.

Can we have the karaoke to ourselves?

Yes, for groups of 20 or more. Exclusive use is €20 per person, paid in advance.

How do we book private karaoke?

Call +31 6 266 325 34, email info@cafechacha.nl, or use the group planner.

Which nights?

Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday, from 21:00. There is no karaoke on Monday and Tuesday.
