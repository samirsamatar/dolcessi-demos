    About Taco Lindo

    Tiny room. Red neon. Serious tacos.

    Taco Lindo is a small taco spot on the Albert Cuypstraat, the market street of De Pijp. White tiles, string lights, a red neon sign and music that puts you in the mood for tacos.

    The menu fits on one board: four tacos, meat or vegan, three hot things and cold Jarritos. Pink pickled onions on top, lime on the side.

    There’s a second spot too, Taco Lindo West. And if you’ve seen a red tent with flowered oilcloth and a hot plancha: that’s Taco Lindo as well.
