Kakegawa, Shizuoka

Our matcha starts on the soft green tea hills of Kakegawa, in Shizuoka. Before the harvest, the tea rows are shaded from the sun, so the leaves grow deep green and sweet.

Ichibancha, the first harvest of spring: only the youngest, most tender leaves.

How to drink matcha 作法

Four quiet steps. We take three for you.

  1. 篩furui

    Sift

    Sift the powder first. Matcha clumps easily, and sifting is what makes it silky.

  2. 湯yu

    Heat

    Hot, never boiling. Around 75–80 °C keeps the sweetness in and the bitterness out.

  3. 点tateru

    Whisk

    Whisk from the wrist in a quick M or W, until a fine, even foam covers the surface.

  4. 雅miyabi

    Savour

    Drink it straight away, while the foam is still alive. Slowly, in a few sips.

Our story 物語

Paris first, then the Zeedijk.

The journey

Miyabi began as a matcha café in Paris. Amsterdam is our second home: a small spot on the Zeedijk, in the heart of Chinatown.

The name

Miyabi is the old Japanese ideal of refinement: grace in simple things. A bowl of matcha, made with care, is exactly that.

The leaf

Our matcha comes from Kakegawa in Shizuoka, one of Japan’s great tea regions. The 2025 first harvest is almost gone, and the 2026 harvest is coming.
