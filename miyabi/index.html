How we make it

From Powder to Perfect Foam

01篩Sift

Kakegawa matcha goes through a fine sieve. Not a single lump.

02湯Heat

Water just under boiling, so the matcha stays sweet and never turns bitter.

03点Whisk

A bamboo chasen, fast, in a zigzag, until a fine foam forms.

04雅Serve

Straight, over milk, or in a crêpe. Always the moment you order.

Our story

From Paris to the Zeedijk

01 · The journey

Paris first, then Amsterdam.

Miyabi opened in Paris. A year later we brought the same matcha, the same crêpes and the same care to Zeedijk 127.

02 · The name

Miyabi means elegance.

雅 (miyabi) is the old Japanese ideal of refinement: doing simple things beautifully. That is how we want every cup to feel.

雅miyabi · elegance

03 · The leaf

Grown in Kakegawa.

Our matcha comes from Kakegawa in Shizuoka, one of Japan’s great tea regions. The 2026 first harvest has just arrived.
