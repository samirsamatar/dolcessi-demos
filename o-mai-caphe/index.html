Our place

Crunch, herbs and patience.

O Mai Caphe is a small Vietnamese café on Kinkerstraat, in Oud-West. The baguettes come out of the oven in trays, every bánh mì goes into a green basket on leaf-print paper, and the coffee drips at its own pace.

Chilli and sauces stand on every table, so you decide how hot it gets.
