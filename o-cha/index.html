Try it

Toss the wok.

Grab the wok and flick it up: noodles, prawns, egg, sprouts, peanuts and lime go flying. Flick hard and the oil catches fire. That’s wok hei, the smoky breath of a screaming-hot wok.

Five good tosses and your pad thai is done. On Binnen Bantammerstraat, the cooks do it for real.

The eethuis

Small room, busy woks.

Look for the red sign on Binnen Bantammerstraat, just around the corner from Nieuwmarkt. Inside it’s tiny and cosy: red walls, wooden tables and a kitchen in full view, where the cooks keep the woks going all evening.

It’s a family-run place. Plates come out fast, on blue-and-white floral china, and guests keep mentioning the same things: fresh flavours, the chicken fried rice, and the kindness of the ladies who work there.
