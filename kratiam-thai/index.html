The story

Two people. One small kitchen in West.

Kratiam means garlic in Thai, and you can taste it. On the Bos en Lommerweg a team of two cooks every plate to order: noodles, curries, salads and soups, with authentic ingredients and a lot of attention to detail.

It is a small place and plenty of takeaway goes out of the door too, so on a busy night it can take a little longer. Guests say it is worth the wait.

  • Cooked to orderEvery plate is made when you order it.
  • Eat in or take awayCall ahead and pick it up on your way home.
  • Tuesday to Saturday16:00 to 22:00. Closed Sunday and Monday.
