The café

A hidden gem
on the Asterweg

Tall windows, pink velvet chairs, deep green counters, plants everywhere and a row of pink lamp posts lighting the room. Breakfast is served all day, lunch comes on brioche and sourdough, and the matcha is organic ceremonial grade.

Come with friends, or come with your laptop. You order at the table with the QR code and they bring it over. Several tables have a socket, and when the sun is out you can sit outside. There’s even a pool table at the back.
