The place

Modest outside.
Sunny inside.

Behind the glass on Trompenburgstraat: yellow tiles, terrazzo tables, a long wooden table and a monkey in a yellow raincoat, coffee in hand, painted across the wall. Ask about the mural.

Mohamed and Dahlia run the place and love to talk you through the menu. They cook with fresh produce, change the menu with the seasons and buy from suppliers that work fair trade.
