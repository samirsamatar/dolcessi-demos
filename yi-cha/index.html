The shop

Yi Cha means "one tea".

It is the tiny shop with the dark wooden front, red lanterns and a panda on the sign, at number 87, right where the Albert Cuyp market begins.

Inside: the boba bar, a chalkboard top 3 and, according to one reviewer, a store that "smells soo nice". Then the dim sum: bamboo steamers stacked high under a sign that reads "Yi Cha! Delicious Dim Sum!"

People come back for the details: pearls poured with a generous hand, a taste of the mix before the whole cup, sometimes a free size-up.
