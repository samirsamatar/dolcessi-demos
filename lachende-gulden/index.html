About the café

A proper neighbourhood café, right on the market.

De Lachende Gulden sits on the corner of the Dapperstraat and the Von Zesenstraat, with the stalls of the Dappermarkt practically at the door. Coffee from eight, a terrace out front, and by the afternoon it's bitterballen and a biertje.

I

Brown café, wild ceiling

Dark wood, brass and low lamps, and then you look up: the ceiling is plastered edge to edge with film and theatre posters. Ajax shirts on the walls, leopard cushions on the bench.

II

Market-day fuel

Homemade apple pie, Holtkamp croquettes, uitsmijters, tosti's and a fresh homemade soup every day. The menu comes in Dutch and English.

III

Why is the guilder laughing?

The guilder was the Dutch currency until the euro took over in 2002. This one is still laughing. Why? Best to ask at the bar.
