Behind the red door · เรื่องราว

Small room. Busy nights.

Little Thai Prince is a small Thai restaurant at Zeedijk 33A, in the old heart of Amsterdam. Look for the red door, the lantern and the round gold sign.

Inside it's cosy: Thai art on the walls, blue-and-white plates on the tables, and dinner only, every night from five to ten.

Guests say it's usually very busy, sometimes with people waiting outside. Their advice: book ahead.
