Filipino heritage · European craft

More than just bread

Manila Bakery & Café sits on Prins Hendrikkade, right across the water from Amsterdam Centraal, next to the NH Barbizon Palace. The counter holds the Filipino bakes so many people grew up with (ensaymada, pandesal, Spanish bread, ube in every shade of purple) next to European mini cakes and croissants.

You will often find the owner behind the counter herself: recipe creator, baker and the one who says hello. (Y habla español también.)

  • Baked fresh daily
  • Real ube, real good
  • Cosy seats inside
  • A bench outside for people-watching
