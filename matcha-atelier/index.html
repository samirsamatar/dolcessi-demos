The atelier way · your turn

Whisk it properly.

Matcha isn’t stirred, it’s whisked: quick M- and W-shaped strokes with a bamboo chasen, wrist loose, until the big bubbles break into a fine, pale foam. Circles won’t get you there.

The atelier

One round counter, made for matcha.

Matcha Atelier is built around a single curved counter of bamboo with a sage-green top: an iron kettle, bamboo whisks in a row, and your matcha made right in front of you.

Guests who practised tea ceremony in Japan call it the authentic way. Then it goes iced, over strawberry, or into a latte with cow or oat milk.
