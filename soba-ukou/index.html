Our story

Rain and good fortune.

UKOU is written 雨幸: rain and good fortune. Behind the door on the Ruysdaelstraat it is calm — white walls, old brick, paper lanterns and a maple in the corner.

Everything here turns around one thing: soba, made in-house from buckwheat and cut by hand. Do not expect twenty dishes. Expect noodles with bite, a cup of hot buckwheat tea and the smell of a soba shop in Japan.
