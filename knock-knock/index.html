How the stripes happen

Fold. Roll.
Repeat.

3layers
  1. Start with a block of cold butter, wrapped in dough.
  2. Fold it in three, like a letter. Roll it out.
  3. Again. Every fold triples the layers.
  4. Again. Paper-thin butter between paper-thin dough.
  5. Once more. In the oven, the butter turns to steam and lifts every layer.
  6. A sheet of cocoa dough on the outside gives the stripes. The crumbs are on you.

Behind the door

A small bakery with a loud name.

On Van Hallstraat, in the Staatsliedenbuurt. Behind the window with the knock knock logo: a pale oak counter, white walls, a green kettle and trays of striped, laminated pastry.

Visitors call it simple and unpretentious, with a relaxed atmosphere and genuinely friendly staff. One baker even warmed up a pain suisse for someone who came in late in the afternoon.
