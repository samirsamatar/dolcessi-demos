二家

Chengdu street food, Oosterdok views

Mala Wang is new on the Oosterdokskade, in the big glass building on the water — next door to the OBA library, a few minutes from Centraal Station.

Inside: tall windows, round mirrors, copper lamps and an open kitchen, so you can watch your bowl come together. The sign out front says it plainly: 成都小吃 · 家常面 — Chengdu street food, home-style noodles.

Still new, and already a perfect 5.0 from its first 27 Google reviews. Guests mention meeting the owner, a team that makes you feel at home, and the numbing tingle of real Sichuan pepper.
