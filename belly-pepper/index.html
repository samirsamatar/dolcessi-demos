Our place · De Clercqstraat 128

Red brick, white marble and a window you can’t miss.

Belly Pepper is a Turkish-inspired daytime kitchen. Simit and menemen in the morning, the Belly Burger after noon, mantı on Thursdays. Everything lands on white marble, and the tea comes in tulip glasses.

Look for the black-and-white graffiti window on De Clercqstraat. Inside: red brick, old rugs, fresh flowers on the tables and a counter full of cake. Planning something? The window says it too: all day brunch, events & catering.
