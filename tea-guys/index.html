Inside

Blue skies,
indoors.

Step in from the Albert Cuypstraat and you’re under a pastel sky: a cloud mural along the wall, a few comfy seats, shelves of lucky cats and the guy in the sunglasses keeping an eye on the bar.

Behind the counter, the actual tea guys make every cup the way you asked for it: your sugar, your ice, your milk, your toppings.

  • A heart on every lid
  • Sugar 0–100%, your call
  • Oat, soy or coconut milk
  • One free topping, always
  • Ceremonial-grade matcha
  • Open every day, 12–18
