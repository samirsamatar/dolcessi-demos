About the café

A sweet spot in Noord.

Amazing Café is an Asian dessert café on the Buikslotermeerplein. Behind the glass: roll cakes in matcha raspberry, honeydew melon, taro and charcoal coconut, ube and pandan chiffon, and tiramisu in little cups.

At the bar they layer matcha over strawberry or mango, and make milk tea with pearls and really tasty coffee. Guests call it their happy place for a break, and a perfect spot to get some work done.
