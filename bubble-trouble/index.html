The shop

Scoops on one side, boba on the other.

Bubble Trouble is a small ice cream and bubble tea shop at Javastraat 131, in the Indische Buurt. One counter, two kinds of happy.

The ice cream comes from IJscuypje, 'Original Dutch' as the window says, in flavours like kletskop, speculaas and raspberry. The bubble tea goes from classic milk tea to brown sugar boba, taro and matcha. And then there are the macarons.

Vegan, sugar-free or gluten-free? The chalkboard out front has you covered: shakes, ice cream and cones.
