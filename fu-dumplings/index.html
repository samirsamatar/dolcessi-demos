The name

福

Fu means
good fortune.

福 is the first character of 福香慧, Fu Xiang Hui: the name on the sign outside and on the aprons.

The menu is short: six kinds of dumplings, three noodle dishes, a few cold salads. Or as one regular wrote: that’s exactly what makes it special.
