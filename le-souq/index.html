أتاي · atay

Pour it from up high

In Morocco mint tea is poured from high above: the leaves swirl, the tea takes in air, and a good glass wears a crown of foam. Grab the teapot, start low, and lift it while you pour.

  1. 1The first glass is as bitter as life,
  2. 2the second as strong as love,
  3. 3the third as soothing as death.

السوق · the market

Half café, half souq

Souq means market, and that's the other half of the place: shelves of Moroccan ceramics and glassware, pottery, leather bags and straw hats, cosmetics, scented oils, teas and groceries.

Step inside

Somewhere between Bos en Lommer and Marrakesh

Lime-white walls with arched niches, reed walls and bamboo, cushions in olive green and terracotta, a pink brick corner hung with leather bags, and a zellige fountain where a little sign asks you to wash your hands with beldi lemon soap.

On another little sign by the window: keep calm and ch'rab atay. Drink your tea.
